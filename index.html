01 — About

Research grounded in learning, built for impact.

Exploring how intelligent systems learn, adapt, and support people in meaningful settings.

I am Dr. Hidayaturrahman, a lecturer at the School of Computer Science (SoCS), BINUS University. My academic work spans AI and machine learning, with particular interest in deep learning systems that can adapt to changing data and tasks.

My doctoral research investigated domain adaptation for image classification using deep learning and adversarial multitask learning. This work reflects a broader interest in model robustness and how learning systems transfer knowledge across different environments.

Alongside research, I teach computer science and explore ways to connect foundational concepts with current AI practice.
